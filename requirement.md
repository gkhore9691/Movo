# Movo — AI-Powered Business Operating System

## Master Build Prompt — Detailing Studio Edition

You are building **Movo**, a premium, AI-powered business operating system for Indian small and medium businesses.

The first vertical is **automotive detailing studios**, using a fictional/demo business called **Detailing Street — Indore**.

This is NOT a generic CRM.

The product should feel like an **AI business manager + operations command center** that happens to contain CRM capabilities.

The core promise:

> **Movo keeps the business moving.**

The owner should open Movo and immediately understand:

* What is happening today
* What needs attention
* Where money is being lost
* Which customers need follow-up
* Which cars are currently in the studio
* What Movo can handle automatically

---

# 1. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Build the product around these principles:

### 1. Zero training

A non-technical Indian business owner should understand the product within minutes.

### 2. Action over information

Don't overwhelm users with tables and charts.

Every important screen should answer:

> **"What should I do next?"**

### 3. AI should DO things

Do not make AI a decorative chatbot.

Movo should identify opportunities, recommend actions and execute workflows.

### 4. Mobile-first thinking

The owner may use desktop in the office but the salesperson/technician may use a phone.

### 5. Premium, not enterprise

Do NOT make it look like Zoho, Salesforce or an old-school ERP.

The visual benchmark is closer to:

* Linear
* Stripe
* Apple
* modern automotive luxury brands

Clean, premium, spacious, sophisticated.

---

Build the product around these five hero features.
